For international product, design and research teams

Learn how your product is experienced in Brazil

We help global product, design and research teams understand how their product is experienced in Brazil: we recruit and verify the right participants, run the research in Portuguese, and bring back findings in English with the cultural context behind them.

In practice: interviews, usability tests and service walkthroughs with people in Brazil who use, or could use, your product or service.

No time or local resources to recruit participants in Brazil? That's usually where studies stall. We take that part off your plate, and stay with you through the research itself.

Local language matters. So does understanding the context behind what people say and do.

From participant recruitment and screening to Portuguese-language research and English-language findings, yUserLab helps your team learn from Brazilian users without losing the cultural and behavioural nuances that inform better decisions.

  • Recruit and screen participants against your study criteria, across Brazil's regions and profiles, with first participants within days.
  • Conduct research in Portuguese, with language and cultural support that goes beyond literal translation: expressions, regional differences and social norms.
  • Understand local behaviours, expectations and decision-making contexts.
  • Receive findings in English, with cultural context and actionable insights.
  • Go further with service design: map journeys and touchpoints and shape how your service should work for people in Brazil.
  • Leave incentives to us: we pay participants locally and send you a single invoice.

Consultancies and agencies: running a study for a client with users in Brazil? We can handle recruitment, Portuguese-language fieldwork and incentives as part of your project.

    Para empresas brasileiras

    Sua empresa quer crescer em outros mercados? Comece entendendo as pessoas.

    Expandir para outro país exige mais do que traduzir um produto ou adaptar uma comunicação. É preciso compreender como as pessoas vivem, escolhem, compram e utilizam soluções naquele contexto.

    A yUserLab ajuda empresas brasileiras a investigar novos mercados por meio de pesquisa com pessoas locais, identificando necessidades, expectativas, comportamentos, critérios de escolha e possíveis barreiras à adoção.

    • Compreender consumidores em mercados internacionais.
    • Investigar necessidades, hábitos e critérios de decisão.
    • Identificar diferenças culturais e barreiras de adoção.
    • Transformar descobertas em insights acionáveis para decisões de produto, serviço e expansão.
